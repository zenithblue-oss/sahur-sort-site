What you get

Frantic sorting

Swipe each item into the right bin before the timer runs out. Easy to learn, hard to master.

Mini-game collection

Tower climbs, army rushes, colour labs, parking jams and more, each with its own levels.

Shop and rewards

Earn coins to unlock new bins, backgrounds and extras. Daily missions and achievements keep it fresh.

Comic style and juicy sound

Bold colours, chunky outlines, wooden clicks and upbeat music.

Free to play

Ads between rounds, and optional rewarded ads for free coins. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns the between-round ads off.

Cloud save on Android

Sign in with Google Play Games to back up your progress. See the privacy policy.
