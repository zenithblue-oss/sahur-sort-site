Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop, go to Coins & Offers, and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. Restore brings back non-consumable items such as Remove Ads, bundles and the premium pass. Coin packs and hints are consumable and cannot be restored once spent.

I bought something and did not get it.

Wait a minute and check your connection, then tap RESTORE PURCHASES. If it is still missing, report it with the order number from your Apple or Google receipt. Do not post the whole receipt or any payment details.

What does Remove Ads do?

It stops the ads shown between rounds. Rewarded ads, where you choose to watch one for free coins, stay available and are always optional.

How do I get a refund or cancel something?

Apple and Google handle refunds. On iPhone or iPad, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history, or see the Google Play refund help page.

Progress and saves

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes it. On Android, signing in with Google Play Games also backs your progress up to the cloud. The iOS version has no cloud save yet, so progress does not move between iOS devices.

How do I move my progress to a new Android phone?

Sign in to Google Play Games with the same Google account on both phones. The game loads your cloud save when you sign in. If the game finds two different saves, it asks you which one to keep.

Ads

Why are there ads?

They keep the game free. You see short ads between rounds, and you can opt in to rewarded ads for free coins. Remove Ads stops the ones between rounds.

An ad is broken, too loud or not suitable.

Report it, with your device and what the ad showed, if you can. See Contact. A screenshot helps.

What data do ads collect?

See the privacy policy.

Devices

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 15.0 or newer, and Android phones and tablets on Android 8.0 or newer. The App Store version is coming soon. Play it on Google Play today.

The game is slow or crashes.

Close other apps, restart your device, and update the game and your system. If it still happens, report it with your device model, OS version and what you were doing.

Delete my data

The game has no account. To remove local data, uninstall it or clear its storage. On Android you can delete the Google Play Games cloud save in the Google Play Games app under Settings > Delete Play Games account & data. Details are in the privacy policy. To make a data deletion request to us anyway, use Contact below. Ad service data is held by the ad providers and is described in the policy.

Contact

Report a bug or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Include your device, its OS version, the game version if you know it, and what happened.

Issues are public. Do not include personal details, receipts or payment information.
