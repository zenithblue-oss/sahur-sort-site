Privacy Policy

Sahur Sort ("the game", "we", "us") is made by Ivarna. This policy covers the iOS and Android versions of the game.

The short version

  • There is no account to create. You do not give us your name, email or any other personal details.
  • We do not run a server that receives your gameplay data, and we do not sell personal information.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. On Android you can also back it up with Google Play Games.
  • The game shows ads through third-party ad services. Those services collect some device and usage data, described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Data we handle

Game progress (on your device)

The game stores coins, levels, scores, unlocked items, settings, achievements, and the purchase transaction IDs and unlocked items needed to restore what you bought. This is kept in local storage on your device and is not sent to us.

Google Play Games (Android only)

On Android the game can sign you in with Google Play Games to back up your save to Google's cloud-save service and to record achievements. Your save data is then stored by Google and linked to your Google account under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive it. There is no sign-in, cloud save or Game Center link in the iOS version.

Ads (iOS and Android)

The game uses Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) as its ad service, with Unity Ads as an ad network. They show interstitial ads between rounds and optional rewarded ads. People who buy Remove Ads no longer see the between-round ads. To serve and measure ads, these services may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID where your device makes it available (Android advertising ID, or the iOS identifier for vendors);
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • usage and ad interaction data, such as ads shown, clicked or completed;
  • device and technical details such as model, operating system version, language, and crash or performance diagnostics.

The iOS version does not show Apple's app tracking prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. On iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) is therefore not available to the ad services.

We do not use any other analytics or crash-reporting service. Unity's own analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting are switched off in the game.

Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs, Remove Ads, bundles, hints and the premium pass) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle your payment details and receipts under their own policies. We never receive your card or payment details.

Other

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or health data. It has no chat and no user-generated content.

2. How the data is used

  • To save your progress, restore purchases and unlock what you bought (on your device).
  • To sync your save and achievements through Google Play Games (Android).
  • By the ad services: to show, limit and measure ads, prevent fraud, and keep their services working.

4. Your choices

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads, or opt out of ad personalisation there.
  • iOS: manage ad personalisation in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Skip Google Play Games sign-in if you do not want cloud save. The game still works and saves on your device.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop between-round ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

5. Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstall the game, or clear its storage (Android: Settings > Apps > Sahur Sort > Storage). This removes your local progress.
  • Google Play Games cloud save (Android): delete it in the Google Play Games app, under Settings > Delete Play Games account & data, or through your Google Account.
  • Ad service data: we do not hold it. You can ask the providers in section 3 to delete it. Resetting your device's advertising ID unlinks future data from earlier data.
  • Purchases: records are held by Apple or Google.

We do not keep an account or personal data about you, so there is usually nothing further for us to delete. If you want to make a deletion request anyway, see Contact.

6. Children

Sahur Sort has an App Store age rating of 9+ and is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ad services above may collect the device data listed in section 1. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, see Contact.

7. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents may also ask what personal information is collected, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

We do not collect your personal information ourselves and we do not sell it. The data in section 1 is handled by the third parties in section 3. Ask them to act on your request, and use the device settings in section 4. We are happy to help you find the right route; see Contact.

8. Security

Local data stays on your device under your device's own protections. Data held by third parties is protected under their policies. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. The new version will appear on this page with a new effective date above. Continuing to use the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy, or a data request: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so do not include personal details.
